LAKE SEVAN / OUR PRIORITY

Not just a lake.
Armenia's lifeline.

Lake Sevan is Armenia’s largest freshwater reservoir and a treasure of extraordinary environmental, economic and cultural significance.

Its ecosystem supports biodiversity, fisheries, agriculture, recreation and communities. Water quality pressures make sustained observation and evidence-based protection essential. For Water2Data, Lake Sevan remains a central focus of our research and monitoring vision.

01 · Water-quality monitoringDevelop approaches to observe key indicators and their changes over time.
02 · Ecosystem awarenessMake environmental information clearer for communities and researchers.
03 · Data for actionSupport future evidence-informed stewardship and collaboration.
04 · A long-term commitmentKeep Sevan central as our monitoring technology and partnerships evolve.
Explore the water-quality simulation ↗

Background: FAO Armenia and Armenia’s Sevan basin management plan. Our monitoring activities are a project focus, not a claim of lake-wide deployment.
